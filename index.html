What happens to your details

  • What’s kept: your name, your email (from Google), your age range, how you heard about the games, your town if you add it, which games you play and when, and whether you play on a phone, tablet or computer.
  • Why: so only people with a pass can play, and so we can see who’s playing and where people find the games.
  • Emails: only if you tick the box. Change your mind any time with “Change my details” on your pass.
  • Never sold or shared. Details are stored in the games’ database (Supabase). Google only handles the sign-in.
  • Delete any time: “Delete my details” on your pass removes everything, and your pass with it.
  • You need to be 13 or over to get a pass.
